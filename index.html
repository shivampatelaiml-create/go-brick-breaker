HOW TO PLAY

🎮 MOVE THE PADDLE

Use the ← and → arrow keys to move the paddle.

🧱 BREAK THE BRICKS

Hit the bricks with the ball and destroy them to earn points.

⭐ SCORE

Destroy more bricks to increase your score and beat your best score.

🔥 COMBO

Destroy bricks continuously to build your combo and earn more points.

⚡ POWER-UPS

Collect power-ups to gain special abilities and make the game easier.

❤️ LIVES

Don't let the ball fall below the paddle. You have limited lives.

⏸ PAUSE

Press SPACE or the pause button to pause the game.

📱 MOBILE

On mobile devices, use the left and right buttons to control the paddle.
